## Corrections applied

1. Google route input now accepts the millisecond RFC3339 timestamp generated by the browser. Previously it caused HTTP 400. Regression covers that exact format.
2. Itinerary validation rejects a transfer after its destination visit or before its origin visit. Retry guidance explains the chronology constraint.
3. Known route research runs concurrently with general research where its targets are already available. AI waiting UI includes elapsed time; timeout bounds now cover server cleanup. Changing a trip or route during AI generation is guarded.
4. Selecting a different route clears the old route quote and fare. Paid/pending booking protection and explicit owner locks remain enforced.
5. Connections use the selected arrival/departure airport and include the final airport link. Two exact museum identities now have OSM-sourced coordinates; unknown places do not receive fabricated coordinates.
6. Museum admission enrichment reads two fixed official websites. When refresh fails, an independently checked dated reference is available for at most 30 days, retaining its original check date. National Museum foreign adult MYR5; Islamic Arts Museum adult MYR20. These are per-person references, not future availability or a conversion into the IDR subtotal. Other unverified admissions remain unknown.
7. Hotel detail fetches have a bounded timeout and retry control. Transport widget retry resets its timer and has an external affiliate fallback.
8. Budget categories with unknown fares no longer misleadingly show Rp0; partial categories identify missing costs. Explicit verified zero prices remain possible.

Official admission sources: [National Museum](https://www.muziumnegara.gov.my/en/ticket), [Islamic Arts Museum Malaysia](https://iamm.org.my/visit-us). Coordinate sources: [National Museum OSM](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/323394984), [Islamic Arts Museum OSM](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/659878360).
